## Our Educational Model

Bridge Public Schools is built around a clear, deliberate approach to teaching and learning—one designed to prepare every student for success in college and life.

### Our Launch Plan

Bridge Public Schools will open with 200 students—100 kindergarten students and 100 sixth-grade students—and add two grades each year until reaching full K–12 enrollment. This launch design allows Bridge to establish strong early elementary foundations while also creating a clear middle school pathway toward college readiness.

### Instructional Model

The educational model is built around rigorous, standards-aligned instruction; strong early literacy and numeracy; integrated support for multilingual learners; structured school culture; and continuous use of data to improve instruction. In classrooms, this will look like explicit teaching, guided practice, independent application, frequent checks for understanding, student work analysis, and timely intervention. Within the first weeks of school, students will be assessed in reading and math so staff can identify needs and provide appropriate support immediately.

### Supporting Multilingual Learners

Bridge will be intentionally designed to serve multilingual learners. Teachers will integrate language development into academic instruction through vocabulary development, structured academic discussion, sentence frames, content-based language supports, and frequent opportunities for students to speak, read, and write across disciplines. The school will affirm students' home languages and cultures while ensuring students develop the academic English necessary for long-term success.

### School Culture

A visitor walking onto campus should see a warm, orderly, and joyful school: students greeted by name, classrooms with clear routines, teachers checking for understanding, students discussing rigorous texts and problems, and staff using data to provide timely support. The model combines high expectations with strong relationships and consistent systems so students feel safe, known, challenged, and supported.
